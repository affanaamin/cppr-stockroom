🏛️ Centre for Public Policy Research (CPPR)

The Centre for Public Policy Research (CPPR) at the Institute of Management Sciences (IMSciences), Peshawar, is a leading policy think tank dedicated to advancing research and providing technical support to the government on various policy matters.

CPPR specializes in coordinating quantitative and qualitative baselines, impact assessments, perception surveys, third-party validations, and monitoring and evaluation services. With a strong focus on humanitarian and developmental research, the Centre boasts expertise in research design, technical methodologies, data management, and analysis.

📜 Background & Purpose of the Stockroom

Public policy practitioners, researchers, students, academics, government officials, media professionals, development partners, and civil society organizations often require access to a wide range of laws, Acts, rules of business, policies, strategies, and SOPs. However, these resources are generally dispersed across different departments and archives, making retrieval time-consuming and inefficient.

To address this gap, CPPR established the Public Policy Stockroom to create a centralized, organized, and regularly updated repository of Pakistan's public policy and legal documents. It enables users to find relevant information in a few simple clicks, reducing duplication of effort and supporting evidence-based research.

CPPR Research & Policy Thrust Areas

CPPR conducts multi-disciplinary research, legislative analyses, and policy reviews across core public sector domains in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Pakistan.

🏛️ Governance & Local Government

Key Research Domain

Analysis of local council devolution, Tehsil Rules of Business, Village and Neighbourhood Council planning manuals, and provincial administrative structures.

💊 Healthcare & Public Health

Key Research Domain

Health sector baseline surveys, medical university governance charters, healthcare protection acts, and emergency health SOPs.

⚖️ Law, Justice & Human Rights

Key Research Domain

Judicial administration, Civil Courts amendments, Code of Criminal Procedure updates, and statutory commissions on the status of women.

💼 Economic Policy & Public Finance

Key Research Domain

Public procurement regulations (KPPRA), provincial financial rules, mineral sector taking-over acts, and commercial governance frameworks.

🎓 Higher Education & Human Resource

Key Research Domain

University charter legislation, Civil Servants appointment/promotion/transfer rules, and youth development frameworks.
