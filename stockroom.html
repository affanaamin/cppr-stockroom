Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Local Government Act, 2013

An Act to rationalize and reorganize the local government system in the Province of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Act No. XXVIII of 2013 • Enacted: 31st October, 2013 • In Force

Preamble.—WHEREAS it is expedient to encourage local government institutions composed of elected representatives and having special representation of peasants, workers, women, minorities and youth;

AND WHEREAS clause (i) of Article 140A of the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan provides for establishment of a local government system and devolution of political, administrative and financial responsibility and authority to the elected representatives of the local governments;

CHAPTER I — PRELIMINARY

1. Short title, extent and commencement.—

  1. This Act may be called the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Local Government Act, 2013.
  2. It extends to the whole of the Province of the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa except, areas notified as cantonments or any other area excluded by Government through notification in the official Gazette.
  3. It shall come into force on such date as Government may, by notification in the official Gazette, appoint and different dates may be appointed for different provisions of this Act.

2. Definitions.—

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

  1. "building" includes any shop, house, hut, outhouse, shed, stable or enclosure built of any material and used for any purpose, and also includes a wall, well, verandah, platform, plinth, ramp, stair-case and steps;
  2. "bye-laws" means bye-laws made under this Act;
  3. "cattle" means cows, buffaloes, bulls, oxen, bullocks, heifers, calves, camels, sheep, goats and includes any other animal declared by Government to be cattle for the purposes of this Act;
  4. "devolution" means conferment of political, administrative and financial authority upon local governments;
  5. "Local Council" means a Village Council, Neighbourhood Council, or Tehsil Council established under this Act.

CHAPTER II — LOCAL GOVERNMENTS & CONSTITUTION OF COUNCILS

3. Composition of Local Governments.—

The local governments established under this Act shall comprise: (a) City District Government in Peshawar; (b) Tehsil Municipal Administrations; and (c) Village and Neighbourhood Councils across districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

4. Delimitation of Councils.—

The Election Commission of Pakistan shall delimit Village and Neighbourhood Councils according to geographical contiguity, administrative boundaries, and population guidelines.

CHAPTER III — POWERS & FUNCTIONS OF LOCAL COUNCILS

23. Functions of Village and Neighbourhood Councils.—

Every Village Council and Neighbourhood Council shall perform civic duties, supervise primary health and education facilities, maintain municipal hygiene, and register births, deaths, and marriages within its jurisdiction.

24. Community Mobilization & Dispute Resolution.—

Councils may facilitate Musalihat Jarga (conciliation committees) for amicable dispute settlement in petty civil and family disputes in accordance with rules framed under this Act.

CHAPTER IV — FISCAL TRANSFERS & LOCAL FINANCE

53. Provincial Finance Commission (PFC).—

The Government shall establish a Provincial Finance Commission to make recommendations on the equitable distribution of resources between the Provincial Consolidated Fund and local councils.

54. Local Fund & Accounts Audit.—

Each Council shall maintain a dedicated Local Fund subject to annual scrutiny by the Auditor-General of Pakistan.

CHAPTER V — DISTRICT ADMINISTRATION & OVERSIGHT

78. Relationship of Government with Local Councils.—

Government shall supervise and inspect local councils to ensure strict adherence to this Act, relevant statutory rules, and Provincial development priorities.

CPPR Policy Assessment

The KP Local Government Act, 2013 represents the most comprehensive municipal devolution framework enacted in Pakistan post-18th Amendment. However, continuous amendments in 2015, 2019, and 2021 have highlighted recurring tensions between provincial administrative secretariats and elected local council chairpersons regarding fiscal discretion.
