Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Policy Stockroom?

expand_more

The CPPR Policy Stockroom is a comprehensive digital repository of statutory documents, research papers, and policy archives curated for legal professionals, researchers, and public policy analysts to facilitate high-density information retrieval.

How do I export data?

expand_more

Registered users can export statutory texts and metadata in PDF, CSV, and JSON formats. Look for the export icon on any document view or list page to initiate a download.

Who can access the repository?

expand_more

Public access is available for a limited set of historical archives. Full access to current statutes, advanced search capabilities, and bulk export features requires an institutional or individual researcher account.

How often is the data updated?

expand_more

The core statutory database is updated bi-weekly. Amendments, repeals, and newly promulgated ordinances are typically reflected within 48 hours of official gazette publication.

Glossary of Terms

Act

Primary

A law passed by the provincial or national assembly and assented to by the Governor or President, forming the primary source of statutory law.

Ordinance

Temporary

A temporary law promulgated by the Governor or President when the respective assembly is not in session. It holds the same force as an Act but must be ratified within a specific timeframe.

Statutory Rules

Delegated

Detailed regulations made by the executive branch under the delegated authority of a parent Act to facilitate its implementation and administration.

SOPs

Administrative

Standard Operating Procedures; administrative guidelines providing step-by-step instructions for executing tasks consistent with relevant rules and Acts.

Bill

Draft

A draft version of a proposed law presented to the assembly for debate, amendment, and eventual passage into an Act.
