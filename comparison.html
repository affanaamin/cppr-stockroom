KP Local Government Act Comparison

Version A
SECTION 22

Constitution of Tehsil Council

There shall be a Tehsil Council for each Tehsil, consisting of general seats, women, peasants and workers, youth and non-Muslims as provided in this Act.

Repealed SECTION 23

Powers of Tehsil Council

(1) The Tehsil Council shall exercise the following powers and perform the following functions:

(a) to approve taxes, cesses, user charges, rates, tolls and fees proposed by the Tehsil Municipal Administration;

(b) to approve annual budget and appropriation of funds for the Tehsil Municipal Administration.

SECTION 24

Conduct of Business

The business of the Tehsil Council shall be conducted in such manner as may be prescribed by rules.

Version B
SECTION 22

Constitution of Tehsil Council

There shall be a Tehsil Council for each Tehsil, consisting of general seats, women, peasants and workers, youth and non-Muslims as provided in this Act.

Modified SECTION 23

Functions and Powers of Tehsil Local Government

(1) The Tehsil Local Government shall exercise the following powers and perform the following functions:

(a) to approve taxes, cesses, user charges, rates, tolls and fees proposed by the Mayor;

(b) to approve annual budget and appropriation of funds for the Tehsil Local Government;

New Section SECTION 23A

Fiscal Autonomy and Devolution

(1) The Tehsil Local Government shall have financial autonomy to manage its internal revenues.

(2) Provincial finance commission grants shall be directly transferred to the Tehsil Local Government account.
